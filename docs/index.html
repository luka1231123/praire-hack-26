How it works

Three plain steps, with no black-box classifier. Every group comes with the exact posts, timestamps, links, and posting hours behind it.

Step 1

Filter out junk

—

Keeps political posts and strips out porn, casino and crypto ads, and everyday chat.

Step 2

Link repeating pairs

—

Two accounts connect only if they share a link, copied text, or instant repost within seconds on at least 2 separate posts. Viral posts shared by over 100 accounts are ignored.

Step 3

Group teams with Leiden

—

Splits the connected accounts into tight teams of 3 or more, separating suspected bot rings from normal news feeds.

What is Leiden grouping?

Imagine drawing a line between every pair of accounts that keep posting the same link or message. Leiden is a graph-grouping algorithm that splits that web into tight teams, keeping coordinated accounts together without merging unrelated people into one giant blob.

Why this works better than other methods

Other approach Where it fails Why PropNET works better (with a real example)
AI-text detectors Break as soon as someone edits the text, and wrongly flag real people. We ignore writing style and link accounts by shared URLs and timing. Example: 6 Bluesky accounts posting different rewrites of the same nextstamina.com political story 0.1s apart ().
Exact copy-paste matching Fooled whenever each account rewords the talking point. Shared links and timing still connect paraphrased posts. Example: Two X troll accounts (blmsoldier and robertebonyking) rewording the exact same anti-voting reply 60s apart ().
Black-box ML classifiers Hard to audit or prove why an account was flagged. Every group shows the exact posts, timestamps, links, and work hours. Example: 48 Russian accounts on X posting 86% of their tweets during 9am–9pm St. Petersburg hours ().

How accurate is it?

PropNET never sees outside labels while grouping accounts. We ran it blind first, then compared its groups against outside datasets.

Important limit: Posting together shows accounts share tools or a playbook. It does not prove who runs them without outside evidence.
