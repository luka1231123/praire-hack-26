The problem

Fake accounts acting together can make a fringe idea look popular.

25,000
TikTok accounts in one pro-Russian push

Romania annulled its 2024 presidential first round. The candidate went from under 1% in the polls to first place.

Meta's largest takedown ever. The accounts kept office hours in China's time zone.

3.6M
articles from Russia's Pravda network

Published in 2024 to seed AI chatbots, which repeated its claims about a third of the time in one audit.

Method

No text model. We score what accounts do together.

Copy-paste used to give networks away. Now AI rewrites every post, AI-text detectors fail once text is paraphrased, and reworded propaganda makes false claims seem more widely believed. Operators can change their words. They can't easily change their behavior.

Don't ask whether a post was written by AI. Ask whether these accounts are working together.

Five traces we score for every pair of accounts

Same reply targets
They reply to the same messages.
Same timing
They hit the same post, link or tag within 60 seconds of each other.
Same links and tags
They share the same URLs, hashtags, mentions or reposts. Rare ones count most: a link two accounts share outweighs one shared by fifty.
Same template text
Their posts match once case, links, digits, punctuation and emoji are removed.
Same schedule
Their weekly activity lines up hour by hour.

From raw posts to ranked groups

  1. One tableEvery post becomes a row: account, time, text, reply target, links and tags.
  2. Shared keysList each reply target, link, tag and cleaned text, and who touched it.
  3. Score pairsEach shared key adds 1/(sharers − 1). Multiply by schedule match.
  4. Find groupsLeiden clustering pulls out tight groups of three or more accounts.
  5. Rank and explainRank groups by shared content per member, with the evidence.

Beyond the datasets

Runs live on Bluesky

monitor.py reads Bluesky's public firehose, about 40 posts a second with no key needed. It keeps a rolling 24-hour window and refreshes its report every five minutes.

Judges behavior, not opinions

Like Meta's own policy, it flags fake accounts working together. It never scores what a post argues, so it doesn't police viewpoints.

About

Built for prairehack26.

Coordinated networks have already changed real elections, and AI now lets them reword every post so copy-paste checks and AI-text detectors miss them. Behind the Accounts looks for what operators can't easily hide: the same reply targets, timing, links, templates and work schedules.

We test it on two datasets with an answer key, Russian Telegram comments labeled by researchers and Russian IRA troll accounts on X, and run it live on Bluesky.
